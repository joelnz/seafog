Still on Silverstripe 4?

Silverstripe CMS 4 is past its end of life and no longer gets security fixes. Silverstripe 3 has been unsupported for years. If your site runs on either, any newly found vulnerability stays open on your site for good.

Silverstripe 6 came out in June 2025, and version 5 gets security fixes until around April 2027. For most sites on version 4, going straight to 6 is the better use of the budget, so you only pay for one upgrade.

Start with an upgrade review. I look through the code and the modules you depend on, and tell you in writing what has to change, what it will cost, and anything that won't survive the move.

Security comes first

My background is cybersecurity as well as web development. On a Silverstripe site that means admin accounts locked down with two-factor login, old editors removed, file uploads restricted, and security headers set properly. It's the work that tends to get skipped when a site has run for years without much attention.

When Silverstripe isn't the right fit

For a small business that wants to edit its own site and not think about hosting, I usually recommend Squarespace. For web apps and booking systems I also build with Payload CMS. If you're unsure which one suits you, I've compared the common options in Squarespace vs Wix vs WordPress.

Common questions

Is Silverstripe still supported?

Yes. Silverstripe CMS 6 was released in June 2025 and is actively maintained. Versions 3 and 4 are no longer supported.

How much does a Silverstripe upgrade cost?

It depends on how much custom code and how many modules the site uses. A small site with standard modules is a much smaller job than a large one with custom integrations. The upgrade review gives you a fixed price before any work starts.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. I take on contract Silverstripe work for Wellington agencies, either by the hour or as a fixed-price piece of a larger project.

Can you take over a site someone else built?

Yes. I start by reviewing the code and hosting so you know what you've got, then we agree what to fix first.
