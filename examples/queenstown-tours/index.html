Why we start early

On most summer days the lake is calm until late morning, then a northerly picks up and the water gets choppy. Our morning tour is back on the beach before that happens, which is why first-timers enjoy it most.

We provide

  • A guide who knows the lake
  • Stable two-person kayaks, or singles if you'd rather
  • Spray jackets, life jackets and dry bags
  • Hot drinks after the morning tour

You bring

  • Quick-dry clothes you don't mind getting splashed
  • A warm layer, even in summer
  • Sunscreen and a hat
  • Your phone in the dry bag for photos
