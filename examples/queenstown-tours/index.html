If the lake is too rough to go out, we move you to another time or refund you in full.

Why we start early

On most summer days the lake is calm until late morning, then a northerly picks up and the water gets choppy. Our morning tour is back on the beach before that happens.

What to bring

We provide

  • A guide who knows the lake
  • Stable two-person kayaks, or singles if you'd rather
  • Spray jackets, life jackets and dry bags
  • Hot drinks after the morning tour

You bring

  • Quick-dry clothes you don't mind getting splashed
  • A warm layer, even in summer
  • Sunscreen and a hat
