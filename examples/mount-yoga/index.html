Three kinds of class

Every class is colour coded on the timetable, so you can see at a glance how hard it'll be.

Gentle

Slow stretches, long holds and plenty of rest. Yin, beginners and the Friday unwind.

Good for: first-timers, stiff backs, bad weeks

Flow

Moving with the breath from one pose to the next. Warm, steady and a little challenging.

Good for: most people, once you've done a few classes

Strong

Faster vinyasa with strength work, arm balances and a proper sweat.

Good for: regulars, runners, surfers who want more
