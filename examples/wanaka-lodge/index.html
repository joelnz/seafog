Eight rooms above Lake Wānaka, with a stone fireplace and breakfast from our kitchen.Larchwood

A small timber lodge on the hill above the lake.

Eight rooms, one long table and a fire that's lit every afternoon from April to October. Most guests stay three nights and leave wishing it had been five.

    The fire is lit

    Happening now

  5. 6:30pm

    Dinner at the long table

    Happening now

    Three courses on Fridays and Saturdays, a pot of something slow on the other nights. Local pinot by the glass.

  6. 9:30pm

    Stars from the deck

    Happening now

    Wānaka sits in a dark sky area. There are blankets by the door and a telescope on the deck.

From every window you can see the lake, the islands and the mountains behind.

The lodge was built in 1962 from larch felled on the hill. We restored it slowly over four winters and kept the panelling, the stone hearth and the creaky third stair.

Children over ten are welcome. Dogs can stay in the Garden Room.
