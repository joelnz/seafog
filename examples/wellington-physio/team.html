Our physios

Four physios, all registered with the Physiotherapy Board of New Zealand. Book with whoever suits your injury, or with the first one free.

Mere Tapsell

Neck, headaches and jaw

Fifteen years in practice. Mere works with desk workers, musicians and anyone whose headaches start in the neck.

Also: Dry needling, jaw and TMJ, vestibular

Sam Whitlock

Sports and running

Physio for two Wellington club rugby sides. Sam looks after ankles, knees and hamstrings, and gets you back to training with a plan.

Also: Running analysis, return to sport, taping

Ana Ferreira

Back pain and Pilates

Ana runs the clinic's small Pilates classes and sees most of the back pain that comes through the door.

Also: Clinical Pilates, pregnancy back pain

Tom Hollis

Shoulders and after surgery

Tom sees people recovering from shoulder and knee surgery, and works with the surgeons at Wellington's private hospitals.

Also: Post-surgery rehab, frozen shoulder
