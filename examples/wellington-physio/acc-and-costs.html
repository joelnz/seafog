ACC and costs

If you were hurt in an accident, ACC pays most of your physio. You don't need a GP referral: we lodge the claim at your first visit.

Common questions

Do I need to see a doctor first?

No. Physios can register ACC claims directly. We'll refer you to a GP if we think you need a scan or medication.

What do I bring?

Your date of birth, NHI number if you know it, and the date and details of the accident. Wear clothes you can move in.

How many visits does ACC cover?

ACC approves an initial block of visits, usually up to 16 for a new injury. If you need more, we apply for an extension.

The injury happened months ago. Can I still claim?

Usually yes. Tell us when and how it happened and we'll lodge it. ACC decides on older claims, so it can take a few days.

What if I need to cancel?

Give us 24 hours and there's no charge. Late cancellations are $30.
