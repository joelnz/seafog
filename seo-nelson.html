SEO services in Nelson.

Local SEO for businesses in Nelson, Richmond and across Tasman. A small market where a well set up listing and a few good pages can put you near the top.

How search works in Nelson Tasman

Nelson and Richmond sit close enough together that many customers search across both, but Google still treats them as separate places. A business in Stoke or Richmond that only mentions Nelson can miss searches from its own doorstep. Motueka and Golden Bay behave as their own markets again.

There are far fewer businesses competing for search here than in the main centres. That means the basics, done properly, go a long way: a complete Google Business Profile, steady reviews, and a clear page for each service and area you cover.

What my SEO services in Nelson include

  • Your Google Business Profile, with the right categories, service areas, hours and photos, kept current every month.
  • A page for each area you serve, so a search from Richmond, Stoke or Motueka finds a page written for that place.
  • Local listings tidied up, so your name, address and phone number match on Finda, Yellow, NoCowboys and the directories in your trade.
  • A simple way to ask for reviews after each job, following Google's rules.
  • Technical fixes on your site: speed, page titles, broken links and the settings that stop pages showing up.
  • A monthly report in plain English: where you rank, how many people found you, and what I did that month.

The Nelson businesses this works best for

  • Trades and home services covering Nelson, Stoke and Richmond.
  • Tourism and hospitality, where visitors search before they arrive and reviews decide the booking.
  • Wineries, makers and food producers selling direct, locally and online.
  • Clinics and professional services, where people search by suburb and check reviews first.
