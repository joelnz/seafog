Squarespace SEO checklist.

Eighteen things to check on a Squarespace site, in the order I'd do them. Most take a few minutes, and together they cover the technical side of SEO for a small business.

Before you launch

  1. Choose your plan. Core or above lets you add your own code, which you need for custom structured data. Basic covers everything else on this list.
  2. Connect your own domain and make sure the version without "www" and the version with it both land on the same site.
  3. Write a page title for every page in its SEO settings: what the page is about, then where, then your business name. For example, "Electrician in Lower Hutt | Your Business".
  4. Write a description for every page. One or two plain sentences saying what someone will find there. This is often what shows under your link in Google.
  5. Tidy each page's URL so it's short and readable, such as /electrician-lower-hutt.
  6. Give each service its own page. One "Services" page can only rank for one thing.
  7. Add alt text to every image, describing what's in it.
  8. Resize big photos before uploading. Anything straight off a phone slows the page down.
  9. Delete the template's demo pages, or hide them from search in their page settings.

When you launch

  1. Set up 301 redirects in URL mappings if you're replacing an old site, from every old address to its new home. This is what keeps your existing rankings.
  2. Connect Google Search Console and submit your sitemap, which Squarespace keeps at yoursite.co.nz/sitemap.xml.
  3. Set up your Google Business Profile with the same business name, address and phone number as your site. Here's how to set up a Google Business Profile in NZ.
  4. Check the site on your phone. Google ranks the mobile version first.

In the first month

  1. List your business on local directories such as Finda, Yellow and NoCowboys, with exactly the same details everywhere.
  2. Ask your first customers for Google reviews, using the review link from your Business Profile.
  3. Check Search Console for errors, such as pages Google can't reach.

Every month

  1. Look at what people searched to find you, in Search Console, and write or improve a page for anything you're close to ranking for.
  2. Keep your Business Profile current: hours, photos, and replies to every review.

Common questions

Does Squarespace do SEO automatically?

It handles the technical basics: SSL, a sitemap, mobile layouts and clean code. The rest of this checklist, like page titles, descriptions, reviews and listings, is up to you.

How long does SEO take on a new Squarespace site?

Usually two to six months for local searches, depending on competition. Your Google Business Profile can start showing within weeks.
