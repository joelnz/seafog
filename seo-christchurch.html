SEO services in Christchurch.

Canterbury has more businesses searching for search help than anywhere else I work, and a lot of very good companies with websites that have not been touched since the rebuild.

The rebuild built companies, not pipelines

Canterbury's construction, engineering and trade sector grew through a decade where work arrived without anyone having to go looking for it. Referrals, repeat clients, insurers and main contractors kept the diary full.

That is a wonderful problem right up until the day it stops. A lot of genuinely excellent Christchurch businesses now have a fifteen year trading history, a solid reputation in a circle of people who already know them, and almost no way for somebody new to find them. The website was built in 2013, the Google listing has the old address, and nobody has ever checked what customers actually type.

That is the most common job I do in Christchurch, and it is usually not complicated. Fix the listing, work out the real search terms, build pages that answer them, and let a fifteen year reputation finally show up in the results.

Christchurch is four search markets, not one

The city is flat, spread out and car dependent, and the districts around it have grown faster than almost anywhere in New Zealand. Rolleston has roughly tripled in twenty years. That geography changes what ranking means.

A business in Hornby competing for a customer in Rangiora is not really in the same market, and Google knows it. Searches from Selwyn return Selwyn businesses. If you serve the wider region, "Christchurch" on its own will never reach most of your customers.

  • Christchurch city, from Riccarton and Addington through Sydenham, Merivale and Papanui
  • Selwyn, where Rolleston, Lincoln and Prebbleton are still growing quickly
  • Waimakariri, meaning Rangiora and Kaiapoi, which search as their own towns
  • Rural Canterbury, where the customer is often on a slow connection and a heavy site simply loses them

Which of those you need depends entirely on where you will actually drive. Paying to rank in a district you will not service is the most common waste of money in local SEO, and I would rather build you two pages that work than six that do not.

Common questions

Do I need to rank in Rolleston and Rangiora separately?

If you service them, yes. Selwyn and Waimakariri return their own local results, so a page that only says Christchurch will not reach a searcher in Rolleston. If you do not drive out there, ignore them, because ranking in a district you will not service is wasted money.

My website is from the rebuild era. Fix it or replace it?

Usually replace it, and usually for less than you expect. A site that old is generally slow on a phone, hard for you to edit and structured in a way search engines struggle with. Patching it costs nearly as much as a $2,400 rebuild that you can then run yourself.

Are you actually based in Christchurch?

No. I am in Wellington and work with Canterbury clients over video call. I will not set up a virtual office to fake a local listing, because that breaches Google's guidelines and the suspension lands on your business, not mine.
