Why Squarespace, and when it isn't right.

Squarespace is the right answer for most small New Zealand businesses: it's stable, it doesn't need patching every month, and you can change your own prices and photos without calling anyone.

It's the wrong answer if you need a complex booking engine, a large product catalogue with unusual rules, or something genuinely custom. I'll tell you that at the quote stage rather than three weeks in, and I can build the custom thing if that's what it takes.

  • No plugin stack to maintain, and far less to go wrong than WordPress
  • You get an editor you'll actually use, not a CMS you'll be frightened of
  • Hosting, SSL and backups are handled, with no separate invoices
  • I write custom code where the platform stops, so it doesn't look like a template
