SEO services in Palmerston North.

Local SEO for businesses in Palmerston North, Feilding and across Manawatū. A city with steady search demand and much less competition than Wellington.

How search works in Manawatū

Palmerston North is a regional centre, so customers come from well beyond the city: Feilding, Ashhurst, the wider Manawatū and as far as Levin. Google treats Feilding as its own place, so a business serving both needs to say so on its site and its listing.

The city also has a large student population around Massey University, which means a steady flow of new residents searching for services they've never needed in Palmerston North before. Being easy to find when they first search matters.

What my SEO services in Palmerston North include

  • Your Google Business Profile, with the right categories, service areas, hours and photos, kept current every month.
  • A page for each area you serve, so a search from Feilding or Ashhurst finds a page written for that place.
  • Local listings tidied up, so your name, address and phone number match on Finda, Yellow, NoCowboys and the directories in your trade.
  • A simple way to ask for reviews after each job, following Google's rules.
  • Technical fixes on your site: speed, page titles, broken links and the settings that stop pages showing up.
  • A monthly report in plain English: where you rank, how many people found you, and what I did that month.

  • Rural and agricultural services selling to farms across the region.
  • Hospitality and retail reaching students and new residents.
