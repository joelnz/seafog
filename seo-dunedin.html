SEO services in Dunedin.

Local SEO for businesses in Dunedin, Mosgiel and across Otago. A spread-out city where being found in your own part of town matters.

How search works in Dunedin

Dunedin is long and hilly, and customers tend to look for help close to home. Mosgiel searches as its own town, and the Otago Peninsula and Port Chalmers behave differently again. A listing set up for the central city may not reach someone in Mosgiel at all.

The University of Otago brings thousands of new students and staff every year, who search for everything from flatting services to physios. Visitors heading for the Peninsula search too, mostly on their phones.

What my SEO services in Dunedin include

  • Your Google Business Profile, with the right categories, service areas, hours and photos, kept current every month.
  • A page for each area you serve, so a search from Mosgiel, South Dunedin or the Peninsula finds a page written for that place.
  • Local listings tidied up, so your name, address and phone number match on Finda, Yellow, NoCowboys and the directories in your trade.
  • A simple way to ask for reviews after each job, following Google's rules.
  • Technical fixes on your site: speed, page titles, broken links and the settings that stop pages showing up.
  • A monthly report in plain English: where you rank, how many people found you, and what I did that month.

The Dunedin businesses this works best for

  • Trades and home services covering the city, Mosgiel and the hills.
  • Tourism and hospitality on the Peninsula and in the city.
  • Student-facing businesses: food, fitness, services and retail.
  • Clinics and professional services, where people search by area and read reviews.
