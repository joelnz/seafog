How a Squarespace project works, step by step

  • A call, twenty minutes to work out what you need and whether I'm the right person
  • A fixed quote and a start date, in writing, before any work happens
  • Design first, with one round of changes, so nothing gets built twice
  • Build and content, usually one to two weeks
  • Launch, then thirty days of fixes included

Most sites are live in two to four weeks from the day your content is ready. The usual hold-up is waiting on text and photos, so get started on those early.

When I'll tell you not to use Squarespace

It's the right answer for most small New Zealand businesses. It's the wrong answer for some, and I'll tell you when I quote.

If you need a complicated booking engine, a large catalogue with unusual pricing rules, or a genuine web application, Squarespace will fight you. I build custom sites too. That's a different quote and a longer timeline, and I'll be straight about which one you're looking at.
