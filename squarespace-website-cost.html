2. Squarespace's own subscription

This goes to Squarespace, not to me. You pay it directly and own the account, which means you're never locked to me for hosting.

Roughly NZ$30–70 per month depending on the plan, with commerce plans costing more. Annual billing is cheaper than monthly. Squarespace changes its pricing periodically, so check squarespace.com/pricing for today's figure, and I'll confirm the exact plan you need in the quote.

That subscription covers hosting, SSL, backups and updates. On a WordPress site those are separate line items and separate things to go wrong.

Common questions

Why is a designer cheaper on Squarespace than WordPress?

Because a large chunk of a WordPress build is plumbing: hosting, security, plugin conflicts, updates. Squarespace handles that, so the money goes into design and content instead of maintenance you can't see.

Can I not just build it myself?

Yes, and some people should. Squarespace is genuinely good DIY software. You're paying a designer for the things templates don't give you: a layout built around what you actually sell, page structure that ranks, and someone to blame when it breaks. If your budget is tight, build it yourself and hire me for the SEO.

How long does it take?

Two to four weeks for most sites, from the day I have your content. The content is usually what holds it up, so I'll help with that if you want.

Do I own it?

Yes. The Squarespace account, the domain and the content are all in your name. I get access, not ownership.
