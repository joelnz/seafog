2. Squarespace's own subscription

This goes to Squarespace, not to me. You pay it directly and own the account, which means you're never locked to me for hosting.

Squarespace charges New Zealand customers in NZ dollars. These are the four plans as listed on squarespace.com/pricing when I checked on 14 October 2026:

Basic

Fine for a brochure site. No code injection, and a 2% fee on every online store sale.

NZ$26 / month monthly

The plan most small businesses end up on. Custom code, no store transaction fee, Google Workspace email available.

NZ$31 / month yearly

Plus

Lower card fees (2.5% + 30c). Worth it once a store is doing steady sales.

NZ$49 / month yearly

Advanced

The lowest card fees (2.3% + 30c) and full commerce tools. For busy online stores.

For a typical small business that means NZ$31 a month on Core, paid yearly, which is about NZ$372 a year. Paying monthly costs around 40% more. Card payments through Squarespace Payments start at 2.7% + 30c on Basic and Core. Squarespace changes its prices from time to time, and I'll confirm the right plan for you in the quote.

That subscription covers hosting, SSL, backups and updates. On a WordPress site those are separate line items and separate things to go wrong.

Common questions

Why is a designer cheaper on Squarespace than WordPress?

Because a large chunk of a WordPress build is plumbing: hosting, security, plugin conflicts, updates. Squarespace handles that, so the money goes into design and content instead of maintenance you can't see. I've compared all three, with NZ prices, in Squarespace vs Wix vs WordPress.

How much does SEO cost on top?

Mine is $1,500 to set up and from $600 a month. Most NZ agencies charge $1,000 to $3,000 a month. There's a full breakdown in how much SEO costs in New Zealand.

Can I not just build it myself?

Yes, and some people should. Squarespace is good DIY software. You're paying a designer for the things templates don't give you: a layout built around what you actually sell, page structure that ranks, and someone to blame when it breaks. If your budget is tight, build it yourself and hire me for the SEO.

What if I need something that isn't in the quote?

I charge $100 an hour for anything outside a fixed quote, and I'll tell you the estimate before I start. Nothing gets added to your bill without you agreeing to it first.

How long does it take?

Two to four weeks for most sites, from the day I have your content. The content is usually what holds it up, so I'll help with that if you want.

Do I own it?

Yes. The Squarespace account, the domain and the content are all in your name. I get access, not ownership.
